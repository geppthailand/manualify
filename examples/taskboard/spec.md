## Brief

**Who:** a team lead at a 4-person team.
**Problem:** tasks live in chat threads; nobody sees what's done this week.
**The demo must land three moments:**
1. One board shows every task by stage (to do / doing / done).
2. Adding a task takes seconds (title, assignee, due day).
3. Moving a card to Done counts toward a visible weekly goal — and the report shows the trend.

**Out of scope for the demo:** login, notifications, comments, permissions.
**Open question for the customer:** is "weekly goal" per team or per person?

## Story (what the viewer is told, in order)

1. One board shows every task by stage: to do, doing, done
2. Add work in seconds: a title, who owns it and when it's due
3. The new task lands in To do, owned by Ava
4. Drag a card to Done when it's finished
5. Every finished task counts toward the weekly goal
6. Reports show the trend week by week, and who has what open
